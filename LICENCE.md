## 🧩 About

**Factorio Helper** is a comprehensive helper tool for Factorio featuring overlay, resource tracker, blueprint manager, quick crafting, map reveal, and more.

Based on popular tools like **Factorio Cheat Sheet**, **Helmod**, and **Blueprint Designer**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Helpers
- Overlay — in-game HUD with production stats and alerts
- Resource Tracker — live tracking of resources and bottlenecks
- Quick Crafting — craft items instantly without manual steps
- Map Reveal — reveal entire map for exploration
- Speed Control — adjust game speed (0.5x to 3x)

### 🏗️ Building & Blueprints
- Blueprint Manager — save, load, and share blueprints
- Instant Blueprint — place blueprints without construction bots
- Copy & Paste — enhanced copy-paste with mirroring
- Underground Belt Helper — auto-connect underground belts

### 🚂 Logistics & Automation
- Train Manager — manage trains and schedules easily
- Circuit Network Helper — visualize circuit signals
- Auto-Research — automatically queue research
- Inventory Editor — add or remove items from inventory
